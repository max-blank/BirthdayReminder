## ✨ Overview

Birthday Reminder is a lightweight application designed to make birthday management easier.

The application displays upcoming birthdays, highlights birthdays happening today, and provides a clear overview of birthdays for the current and following month.

Its goal is simple: help users remember important dates without relying on scattered notes, calendars, or manual reminders.

It is built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Electron, and is available as a Windows executable.

## 🎯 Why use Birthday Reminder?

Remembering birthdays can be difficult, especially when important dates are spread across different calendars, social networks, or contact lists.

Birthday Reminder provides a simple and centralized way to:

- ✅ Keep all birthday dates in one place
- ✅ Quickly and simply see upcoming birthdays
- ✅ Get the time to prepare birthday messages or gifts in advance
- ✅ Avoid forgetting the birthdays of friends, family members, or colleagues
